Google is a technology company founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin.

Google is headquartered in Mountain View, California, United States.

The company is best known for its Google Search engine, which helps users find information on the internet.

Google also provides products and services such as Gmail, Google Maps, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Cloud, Android, YouTube, and Google Chrome.

Google develops technologies in areas including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, cloud computing, online advertising, and software.

Google's mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful.
